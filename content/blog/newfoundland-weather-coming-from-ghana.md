One thing I’m definitely still adjusting to in Newfoundland is the weather.

Coming from Ghana, where the climate is generally warm and predictable, Newfoundland feels like the weather has its own personality. You can leave home thinking the day looks fine, then suddenly get wind, rain, cold, and maybe a bit of sunshine all within a short time.

In Ghana, I mostly worried about whether it would rain. Here, I’m learning to check the temperature, wind, “feels like,” and sometimes the forecast twice before stepping outside. 😄

It’s a small part of adapting to a new country, but it has made me appreciate how much climate shapes daily life, clothing, transportation, and even how people plan their day.

For those who moved to Newfoundland from a warmer country, what was the hardest part of adjusting to the weather?
