# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. This is a small personal project, but the same basic standards
apply to any shared space.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Focus on the work — critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith and be welcoming to first-time contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, and
any other project communication channel.

## Enforcement

Reports can be sent privately to **gotinkorang@gmail.com**. Reports will be
reviewed in confidence, and the maintainer may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, issues, or pull requests that violate this code, and may
block repeat or serious offenders.
